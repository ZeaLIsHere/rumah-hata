# Brainstorming Skill

Use this skill when exploring new features, refining architecture, finding edge cases, or creating differentiators against competitors in hackathons and tech competitions.

## Guidelines

1. **Problem-First**: Always anchor ideation in user pain points and real-world metrics, not technology for the sake of technology.
2. **First-Principles Thinking**:
   - What is the root cause of this inefficiency?
   - What assumptions are existing solutions making that might be wrong?
   - What is the minimal intervention that produces 80% of the outcome?
3. **Competition Differentiation Matrix**:
   - Existing Market Solutions (What they do)
   - The Critical Gap (What they miss)
   - Our Unfair Advantage (How we win)
4. **Feasibility vs. Impact Scoring**:
   - High Impact + High Feasibility = Build in Phase 1 (Core MVP)
   - High Impact + Low Feasibility = Prototype / Mock in Phase 2
   - Low Impact + High Feasibility = Fast wins or skip
   - Low Impact + Low Feasibility = Eliminate immediately
5. **Output Format**:
   - Provide concrete, prioritized proposals with clear rationale, tradeoffs, and next steps. Avoid vague bullet points.
