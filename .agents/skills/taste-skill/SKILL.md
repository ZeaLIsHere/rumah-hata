# Taste Skill (Frontend Craft & Art Direction)

Use this skill when designing or refining websites and app screens to replace generic AI defaults with intentional art direction.

## Core Tenets

1. **Idea from the Subject**:
   - The concept must emerge from what belongs exclusively to this subject (its real materials, geographic footprint, numbers, constraints), not generic category templates.
   - The competitor test: If the nearest competitor's name can be swapped into the headline or concept without change, the idea is generic.
2. **Substance Over Restraint**:
   - Removing ornaments is only half the work; an empty layout looks thin and unfinished.
   - Replace clutter with scale: large confident typography, full fields of deliberate color, real photographs or authentic interface fragments, and complete content delivery.
3. **App Screens vs. Marketing Pages**:
   - **App Screens (Dashboards/Admin)**: Quiet neutral borders, tabular aligned numbers, colors reserved strictly for status/data, compact navigation.
   - **Marketing Pages**: One dominant visual anchor per screen, generous section spacing (120-240px), bold asymmetric balance inside a strict grid.

## House Rules (Enforced)

- **Headlines**: Two lines or fewer at every screen width.
- **Clear Title**: Single, clear `h1` answering what the product is on the first viewport.
- **Equal Margins**: Balanced outer padding on left and right; never push everything to one side with an empty void opposite.
- **No Eyebrow Chips**: Drop pill badges parked right above headings unless they convey functional status.
- **No Monospace Outside Code**: Use clean text fonts; reserve monospace strictly for code blocks and IDs.
- **No Gradients as Backgrounds**: Prefer solid, confident ground tones or authentic photography.
- **No Floating Shadows at Rest**: Shadows only on elements that truly float (menus, modals).
- **Two Typefaces Maximum**: One display/heading face, one body face.
